## Nickel 1.0.1

> **Heads up:** Nickel now requires **Android 17** (previously Android 10). The frosted glass needs Android 17 QPR2; earlier Android 17 builds get the same look with solid surfaces.

### New
- **Frosted glass.** The + button, header buttons, coin logo and income/expense pills are now tinted glass that blurs whatever moves behind them. The entry sheet is a glass panel too, and the screen frosts over behind it as it grows out of the +.
- **Automatic backups.** Pick a folder and Nickel saves a dated CSV every 1, 2 or 3 days, keeping the newest 7. No storage permission needed. Find it under Settings → Your data, along with a "Back up now" button.
- **Widget shortcut.** Tapping the home-screen widget opens a new entry with the keypad ready.
- **Better notification capture.** Google Wallet tap-to-pay purchases are now picked up, amounts in other number formats (like `1.234,56 €`) are read correctly, and a payment announced by two apps (say, Wallet and Cash App) only counts once.

### Improved
- The + button is bigger and always stays put while you scroll.
- You can scroll the list right away while the entry sheet is still closing.
- Pulling the entry sheet closed keeps your finger's speed instead of stalling.
- The import dialog's text no longer jitters while the card resizes.
- The list now runs edge to edge, under the status bar.
- The settings and back buttons tick when pressed.

### Fixed
- Swiping a transaction toward "duplicate" and dragging it back no longer duplicates it anyway, and can no longer leave the row ignoring taps.

### Removed
- Pull-to-refresh. It didn't add anything: the ledger already refreshes whenever you return to the app.
- The undo snackbar after deleting a transaction. Swipe left deletes immediately.
