## Nickel 1.0.2

### New
- **Today widget.** A second home-screen widget shows today's net total and your latest few transactions from today, with the time each one happened. It rolls over to the new day at midnight. Like the Balance widget, tapping it opens a new entry.

### Improved
- **Smarter notification capture.** Captured payments now land in the category you last used for that name, the same way typing a note in the entry sheet does, instead of always going to "Other." When Google Wallet's receipt fills in the store name after a card app's alert, the category is guessed again from the store name, but never over a category you've already picked.
