## Nickel 1.0.3

### New
- **Pick which apps auto-add listens to.** A new **Apps to watch** card under **Settings → Automation** opens a picker with search, per-app checkboxes and a Select all switch. Notifications from apps that aren't picked are ignored.
  - If you already had auto-add on, every app starts out picked, so nothing you were capturing from stops working. If you turn auto-add on for the first time, only apps that look like banks, payment apps or text-message apps are picked.
  - Newly installed banking and payment apps join the list on their own, and Nickel posts a "Now watching" notification so you can take them back out. An app you've unticked stays off.
  - Nickel only sees apps that have a launcher icon. It doesn't ask for the "see all apps" permission.
- **Choose what a widget tap does.** Tapping a home-screen widget still opens a new entry by default. Turn off **Settings → Automation → Open a new entry from the widget** to have a tap just open the app instead.

### Improved
- **Smaller widgets.** The Balance and Today widgets now start at 2×1 instead of 3×2, and both can shrink to that size. At one row they show just the total, centered.
- With nothing logged yet, the widgets no longer cut off their empty message. One-row widgets use a shorter version ("Nothing today").
- Moved to the stable Material 3 Expressive components. Settings and transaction rows use the new segmented lists. In the currency and backup interval dialogs, your current choice is highlighted. Checkboxes use the current Material 3 style.
- Accessibility: the Home filter bar is now read out as a single group of choices.

### Fixed
- The Settings footer showed version 1.0.1 even on 1.0.2. It now always shows the version you have installed.
